# Research: survey findings (n = 22, Nov 2026)

Every design decision in this prototype traces back to a finding below.
In the code, look for comments like `// Research: F-CROWD`.

> Note on sample size: 22 people answered the survey. The questions about the
> "What to expect" card were answered by **21** people (one skipped), so those
> figures use n = 21.

## Who answered

- 12 of 22 use Urban Sports Club, 4 have a single-gym membership, 1 uses another multi-studio app, 5 have no membership.
- Ages: mostly 25–54.

---

## Findings and what they mean for the design

### F-PROBLEM: the problem is real
- 19 of 22 have looked at a class and decided not to book it (12 "often", 7 "sometimes").
- 16 of 22 have left a class early or never gone back to a studio (7 "often").

**Design:** Information has to be there *before* booking, on the class detail screen, the step just before "Book".

### F-INFO-GAP: people can't find what they need
- How often people find the info they need before booking (0–10): average **6.2**; 9 of 22 answered 5 or lower.

**Design:** One standard card on every class, always in the same place and order, so nobody has to dig for information.

### F-UNSURE: "Not sure what the class is like" is the #1 barrier
- What stops booking: not sure what the class is like (12), not sure about the level (9), too tired / low energy (8), studio too busy or loud (8), location (8), don't know anyone (6), time (5), price (4).

**Design:** The card answers one question: "what will this feel like?". It uses an icon, a short label and a plain-language value, not long text.

### F-VIBE: atmosphere is the most important factor
- General atmosphere / vibe: 18 "very important" + "dealbreaker" (4 dealbreaker).

**Design:** A one-line plain summary ("vibe line") at the top of the card.

### F-LOCATION: location and time matter
- Location and time: 16 very + dealbreaker (4 dealbreaker); location stops 8 from booking; time stops 5.

**Design:** Class list rows show district, time and duration up front.

### F-CLEAN: cleanliness is the strongest dealbreaker
- Cleanliness: 16 very + dealbreaker, **10 dealbreaker** (the highest).
- 7 left a class or never returned because it was not clean.
- Wishes: air flow / AC, temperature, smell or humidity.

**Design:** "Clean & air" is the second row on the card and is always visible. It shows when the room was last cleaned, a member rating, ventilation and temperature.

### F-CROWD: crowd size is the second-strongest dealbreaker
- Number of people: 13 very + dealbreaker, **9 dealbreaker**.
- Too crowded is the #1 reason people left (9).
- "Studio seems too busy or loud" stops 8 from booking.
- Wishes: how many people usually come at that time, room size, space to move freely, how crowded the changing room gets.

**Design:** "Crowd" is the first row on the card. It shows the typical number of people for this time slot, room size, and a calm / moderate / busy indicator (icon + word, not colour alone).

### F-LEVEL: level labels are not honest
- Level of difficulty: 14 very + dealbreaker (4 dealbreaker); "not sure about the level" stops 9; "too hard" made 3 leave.
- Quotes: "open level" classes where most people are advanced; "beginner" that isn't really beginner; a dance class where you were supposed to already know the choreography.

**Design:** The card shows the official label next to "what it really means" and the prior knowledge needed. It also shows the share of first-timers who said the level matched.

### F-SOUND: music volume and mic
- Music volume: 11 very + dealbreaker (5 dealbreaker); 5 left because the music was too loud.
- Wishes: music volume, whether the trainer uses a microphone, noise.

**Design:** Volume on a 1–5 scale with words ("Quiet background" … "Club-loud"), plus mic yes/no.

### F-LIGHT: light
- Light: 9 very + dealbreaker (4 dealbreaker); 5 left because the light was too bright.
- Wishes: flashing, white, too bright.

**Design:** Light is shown as dim / natural / bright / flashing. Flashing or strobe light is always stated clearly. The app itself never flashes or animates aggressively.

### F-TRAINER: trainer style and touch
- Trainer: 13 very + dealbreaker; "didn't like the trainer" made 8 leave (#2 reason).
- Wishes: style (strict or relaxed), experience, whether they correct you by touching you. One respondent was touched without being asked.

**Design:** A strict ↔ relaxed scale, plus hands-on corrections yes/no and "asks before touching" yes/no.

### F-PARTNER: partner and group exercises
- Partner / group exercises: 9 very + dealbreaker (3 dealbreaker).

**Design:** Partner work is shown as none / optional / required.

### F-FLOW: how the class goes and how hard it is
- Wishes: how the class goes step by step, and how intense it is.
- "Too tired / low energy" stops 8 from booking; "too hard" made 3 leave.

**Design:** A mini timeline (warm-up → main block → stretch) with minutes and intensity for each part.

### F-FACILITIES: facilities
- Wishes: showers (including private showers), lockers, a safe box, space to change, how crowded the changing room gets.

**Design:** A facilities list covering showers (shared/private), lockers, safe box, changing room crowding and towels.

### F-BRING: what to bring and when to arrive
- Wishes: towel, shoes, swimwear; best time to arrive.

**Design:** A "What to bring" section on the card, a best arrival time, and a tickable checklist on the booking confirmation.

### F-SOCIAL: social comfort and leaving early
- "Felt out of place" made 4 leave; "don't know anyone there" stops 6.
- Wishes: feeling stared at, people filming themselves, whether you can leave quietly mid-class without it being awkward.

**Design:** A "Leaving early & comfort" section. It says whether a quiet exit is fine and where the door is, and covers mirrors and filming.

### F-ENERGY: the choice depends on the day *(Version B)*
- 18 of 22: "It depends on my day".
- When tired or stressed: 16 say it changes their choice "a lot", 3 "a bit", 2 skip training; only 1 says no difference.

**Design (B):** A one-tap "How's your energy today?" check-in on the class list. The copy never shames low energy.

### F-SENSORY: there is a sensory-sensitive group *(Version B, and tone in A)*
- 6 prefer calm, quiet places; 6 enjoy busy, energetic places; 5 get tired quickly in noisy or crowded places.

**Design:** Neutral wording. "Loud" or "busy" is information, not a warning, because some people love it. In B, optional sensory preferences decide what gets highlighted.

### F-CARD: the card idea is wanted (n = 21)
- Usefulness (0–10): average **7.3**; 13 of 21 rated it 8 or higher; six 10s.
- 10 would try more new classes, 6 would avoid some classes, 3 not sure, 2 no change. So 16 of 21 say it would change their behaviour.

**Design:** Success metrics: booking rate after viewing a card, "not for me" rate, number of new class types booked, time to decide.

### F-INSIDE: don't build another app
- "The world doesn't need another Urban Sports Club app… all my friends use USC and we book together. Maybe try to improve that app."

**Design:** "Read the room before you go" is a concept feature *inside the Urban Sports Club app*, in its existing booking flow (class list → class detail → book). It is not a standalone app. The prototype uses the app name as plain text only, with no USC logo, colours or visual identity, and is labelled "Concept". Studios and data are fictional.

### F-NOT-TODAY: a soft way to say no
- Follows from F-PROBLEM and F-ENERGY. People often decide against a class, and the decision is about *today*, not the class forever.

**Design:** A quieter "Not for me today" button next to "Book". In A it logs the decision and returns kindly to the list; in B it suggests 2 better-fitting classes.
